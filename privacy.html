Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Information we receive

When you contact us by email, phone or through the contact form, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss potential work.

Contact form

By default, the form opens your email application with a prepared message. If a form service is enabled, your submission is sent through that service to our inbox.

Cookies and third parties

This website does not use advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. Links to LinkedIn and other sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information. We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to respond and maintain business records, and we may share information when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us by writing to jlg@jlg4e.com.
