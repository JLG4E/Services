Product overview

From a real challenge

The challenge

Factories and facilities can have useful data spread across meters, maintenance logs, equipment systems and spreadsheets. Teams may notice failures or waste only after costs rise or production is disrupted.

Our product direction

JLG4E's industrial operations direction is to turn available operational data into understandable trends, anomaly indicators and decision-support insights. A focused initial product should connect to a limited set of data sources and support operators rather than directly control machinery.

Core capabilities

✦

Operational dashboards

Bring key performance indicators and historical trends into one view.

✦

Anomaly indicators

Flag unusual patterns for review using rules and, where suitable, statistical models.

✦

Maintenance insights

Combine equipment history and available signals to prioritize inspections.

✦

Energy analysis

Compare usage over time and identify potential savings opportunities.

✦

Alerts and reports

Send understandable alerts and summaries to relevant teams.

✦

Integration roadmap

Add compatible meters, sensors or industrial systems after validating the initial use case.

A sensible first milestone

Begin with read-only analysis of a customer's existing energy or maintenance records. Prove that the insights are accurate and actionable before adding live equipment integrations.

Initial versions should be decision-support tools. Any connection to industrial control systems requires domain expertise, rigorous testing, safety review and customer authorization.
